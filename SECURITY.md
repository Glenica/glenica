# Security policy

We take the security of Glenica and our users' data seriously.

## Reporting a vulnerability

Please email **support@glenica.com** with the subject line **"Security"**. Do not open a public report.

Include what you found, the steps to reproduce it, and the version you tested (shown on `chrome://extensions`). We will confirm receipt within 3 working days and keep you updated until it is resolved.

Please give us reasonable time to fix the issue before sharing it publicly, and do not access other people's accounts or data while testing.

## Supported versions

Only the [latest release](https://github.com/glenica/glenica/releases/latest) receives security fixes. Please keep your copy up to date.

## How Glenica handles your data

See [glenica.com/security](https://www.glenica.com/security) for every place your data goes, what our servers keep, and why the extension asks for each permission.
